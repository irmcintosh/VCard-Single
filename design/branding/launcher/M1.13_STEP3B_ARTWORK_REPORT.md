### Monochrome source (2,414 bytes)

- **Visible fill:** `#FFFFFF` only. Black appears only inside SVG masks.
- **Effects:** no gradients, filters, glows, or shadows.
- **Background:** transparent; the reference's glowing frame is omitted.
- **Silhouette:** back and front card silhouettes are separated by a knocked-out gap. The V, contact head and body, and two information lines are knocked out of the front card.
- **Bounds:** the artwork scale is 1.95× in board coordinates. Opaque bounds are x 211–803 and y 266–757, inside the centred 626 × 626 px area that corresponds to Android's 66/108 dp adaptive-icon safe zone.

## 5. Differences from the approved design

The SVG is a **faithful approximation, not an exact reproduction**. The flattened 386-pixel source lacks layer and precise curve data.

The following details were reconstructed by visual tracing:

1. **Frame glow:** the reference has a broader, more intense neon bloom, especially at the cyan upper-left and pink-violet lower-right. The reconstruction uses a narrower glow and slightly less saturated corners.
2. **Front-card glow:** the reference's lower-left edge is brighter and more cyan. The reconstruction is cleaner and slightly paler.
3. **V curves:** the arm angles, fold, rounded base, and gradients match closely. The exact curve radii and specular highlights are approximations.
4. **Back card:** the visible top and lower-right edges follow the reference. Its hidden portions are inferred; the visible perspective was preserved using an irregular quadrilateral.
5. **Contact mark and lines:** positions and proportions follow the reference. Colours are sampled approximations.
6. **Monochrome:** the reference's small themed tile shows the card artwork on a dark tile. The source contains only a transparent single-colour foreground; the system supplies the themed background.

No QR code, text, wordmark, or unrelated decorative element was added. The composition was not redesigned.

## 6. Visual-quality limitations for owner review

- **Small-size readability:** at 48 px, the V, layered cards, and contact head remain recognisable. The two information lines become faint. This matches the reference's behaviour at the same size.
- **Launcher masking:** the full-colour master includes the reference's own rounded-square frame. If used unchanged as Android adaptive-icon layers, the launcher mask would add a second shape around that frame. A later implementation step will need to decide whether the glowing frame belongs in the background layer and how it should behave under circular and other masks. This is an implementation decision, not part of this artwork step.
- **Renderer:** the previews have not been compared with an Inkscape render or Android launcher rendering.
- **Owner review:** the owner should confirm that the reconstruction is an acceptable representation of the approved icon before Android implementation proceeds.
