## 4. Consistency findings

### F-01 — Conflicting approval records

**Location:** Header, section 12 and final unnumbered “Approval record.”  
**Finding:** Section 12 says “Awaiting user review” and requests approval. The header and final approval record identify an approved baseline. Two approval headings also make references to “the approval record” ambiguous.  
**Disposition:** Treat P0.1 as approved as explicitly instructed. Preserve the PRD; any future editorial reconciliation of these records requires explicit authorization to modify it.

### F-02 — Stale draft and approval-trigger wording

**Location:** Sections 10–11.  
**Finding:** Section 11 calls the approved document a draft and uses “On approval” language. Section 10 describes P0.1 as requiring approval without recording its completion there. These passages can confuse readers about the current milestone state.  
**Disposition:** Report as editorial/status residue, not a product-scope change. No PRD edits made.

### F-03 — Related-document references cannot be verified

**Location:** Section 11 and the mockup reference in section 8.  
**Finding:** Named source files and the earlier mockup are absent from the available workspace. Their consistency, filename accuracy and accessibility cannot be confirmed. The PRD specifically reports that the Progress document still points to environment setup, but that document's current contents were not independently verified. The unusual punctuation in `01-—-Project-Specification.txt` should be checked against the actual source filename when available.  
**Disposition:** Treat these as unavailable dependencies, not proven broken Markdown links. Do not fabricate or recreate missing source documents.

### F-04 — QR terminology needs contextual care

**Location:** Section 1, FR-03 and FR-04.  
**Finding:** “Contact QR,” “web-profile QR,” “web-link QR” and “Web Link QR” describe two modes. Section 1's general offline QR wording is qualified by FR-04's distinction between offline contact data and an online hosted profile. FR-03's generic reference to QR payloads should be read in the context of contact export; local visibility explicitly does not control hosted content.  
**Disposition:** No conflicting mode requirement identified. Later documentation should consistently distinguish contact QR from web-profile link QR and retain the hosted-content limitation. This review does not select new payload or visibility rules.

### F-05 — Section structure and internal references

**Finding:** Sections 1–12 are present; decisions P0.1-01 through P0.1-12 are unique and sequential; FR-01 through FR-06 are present. The PRD contains product purpose/audience, decisions, journeys, functional and nonfunctional requirements, boundaries, a provisional capability map, unresolved details, planning gates, provenance and approval records. No missing P0.1 section was identified within the requested review scope. References to P0.2, P0.3, P0.5 and P0.6 agree with the deliverables listed in section 10; the P0.1-10 change-policy reference resolves to an existing decision. No internal Markdown anchor links requiring target validation were found.  
**Disposition:** The duplicate approval heading is covered by F-01. The screen map remains provisional and does not approve a navigation hierarchy or ten-screen implementation.

### F-06 — Product requirements and provisional verification detail

**Finding:** No direct contradiction among the twelve decisions and the substantive functional requirements was identified. Offline core operations coexist with optional online profile sharing. Manual app-backup restoration is distinct from deferred existing-vCard import. Future expandability does not authorize multiple profiles in v1.0. Production-quality gates describe future release obligations. Section 6 calls the gates provisional, while section 9 reserves measurement and compatibility details for later planning.  
**Disposition:** Carry the stated requirements and targets forward without claiming they have passed. Clarify methods later within the approved scope; this report does not approve changing targets or adding features.
