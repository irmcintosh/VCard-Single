**P0.5-03 — UI state, navigation and lifecycle**

1. Approval covers only the state pattern and the rule that navigation state holds no payloads.
2. The navigation framework is not approved. It is chosen after FG-00 resolves the toolchain, with a documented fit to the P0.2 flows, stable APIs only.
3. No new route, Settings screen or dialog is introduced.

**P0.5-05 — Asset ownership**

1. Approval covers the ownership principles only; embedded versus sidecar representation is deferred to the P0.5-04/P0.5-06 decision.
2. Stored public-key and image bytes are never re-encoded; the stored digest keeps identifying the retained bytes.
3. Cleanup never deletes a saved asset or an asset pinned by an active export.

**P0.5-08 — QR encoding and rendering**

1. The encoder must provide Model 2, level M, smallest-fit version, ECI 26 only for non-ASCII, and an unscaled matrix. A miss returns to the owner; no truncation, correction downgrade or web fallback.
2. API-31 compatibility and license notices are verified.
3. No scan success is claimed without device and second-scanner evidence.
4. If the candidate fails, alternatives are compared at that point.

**P0.5-11 — Native handoffs and temporary artifacts**

1. Exact artifact versions, manifest entries, provider subclass and paths are approved separately.
2. An external return is never reported as delivery; unknown outcomes never trigger duplicate handoffs.
3. No `.vcf` or contact-image artifact is created before the required S01 confirmation.
4. No broad storage permission, and no claim that earlier external copies can be revoked.
5. The 24-hour unused-retention target never breaks active receiver access.

**P0.5-12 — Cross-cutting safeguards**

1. The threading and concurrency model, per-parser and per-codec limits, permission inventory and measurement method are not approved by this decision.
2. No personal content, key material or file path is logged.
3. The P0.1 targets (usable card within 2 seconds of warm launch; QR within 1 second) remain targets, not measurements.
4. A fixed constraint that proves infeasible is escalated to the owner.

## 14. Provisional fail-closed corruption-recovery policy (2026-10-30)

The project owner approved **fail-closed handling as the provisional owner policy** for unreadable, corrupted, incomplete or invalid saved profile data:

- Do not silently reset affected saved data to an empty profile or expose invalid/incomplete data as a valid profile.
- Do not silently activate an older generation when the previously committed profile is corrupted.
- Preserve unreadable or corrupted data for potential recovery, subject to future explicit retention and storage limits.
- Require an explicit, user-controlled recovery or replacement process consistent with the approved P0.4 manual full-backup and restore workflow.
- Distinguish an interrupted, uncommitted Save from corruption of an already committed generation. Continuing with the last demonstrably committed generation after a proven pre-commit interruption may be valid recovery, not an unauthorized rollback.

This is a policy decision, not an approved detection or recovery mechanism. Exact corruption detection/classification; how to establish the last durable commit; pointer-slot corruption/disagreement; recovery mechanics and preservation limits; whether existing P0.2 UI can display the condition and support recovery, and whether P0.2 change control is required; startup integrity depth; and acceptable durability evidence remain open. ## 15. Provisional two-stage integrity verification policy (2026-10-30)

The project owner approved a **provisional two-stage integrity verification policy** for saved profile storage.

**Stage 1 — startup validation:** Before saved profile metadata is treated as valid, validate active-generation reference integrity and selection subject to the still-unapproved durable pointer protocol; manifest structure and supported schema version; required profile metadata and structural validity; and referenced asset records, expected sizes, digests and ownership/reference invariants. Stage 1 does not prove the integrity of all referenced asset bytes.

**Stage 2 — asset-use validation:** Before an asset is consumed, displayed, exported, shared or otherwise used, verify its expected size and SHA-256 against the committed manifest and apply additional image/OpenPGP validation required by P0.4. Unverified bytes must not be exposed through UI, QR, vCard, Sharesheet, export or backup. Stage 2 corruption follows the provisional fail-closed policy; no silent reset, predecessor substitution or valid-data claim is permitted.

Metadata validity, individual asset integrity and complete profile verification are distinct. A profile is not fully verified while any referenced asset bytes remain unchecked; full-profile verification requires every referenced asset to pass Stage 2. A proven pre-commit Save interruption may recover to the last demonstrably committed generation and remains distinct from corruption of a committed generation.

Verification timing and scheduling, cache validity/invalidation, failure and retry handling, startup performance, coverage of every asset-access path, and the precise boundary between metadata-valid and fully verified states remain technical questions. No verification mechanism, cache, failure UI or P0.2 change is approved. The policy does not approve the A/B pointer or durable commit behavior. P0.5-04 and P0.5-06 remain **PENDING**; P0.5-05 remains **CONDITIONALLY APPROVED for ownership principles only**; FG-00 through FG-07 remain **OPEN**; P0.5 remains **PARTIALLY APPROVED / INCOMPLETE**. Previously approved Android migration exclusions and the P0.4 manual full-backup/restore workflow are unchanged. No implementation or verification phase is authorized.

## 16. Provisional predecessor-retention policy (2026-10-30)

The project owner approved retaining **exactly one previously committed predecessor generation alongside the active generation during normal operation**. Preserve all immutable manifests and asset blobs reachable from either retained generation, as well as assets and generated artifacts required by active export or sharing operations.

Cleanup is permitted only after the new commit is established, generation references are reconciled, and protected assets/artifacts/evidence are identified. Cleanup must be idempotent and must not delete active, predecessor, pinned or otherwise protected data, nor corrupted data or recovery evidence protected by the fail-closed policy. Do not automatically activate a predecessor in response to corruption of committed data.

One predecessor is the normal retention target, not an unconditional maximum during interrupted operations, corruption investigation, or protected artifact lifetimes. Exact normal storage budget/limits; low-storage behavior during Save, restore and cleanup; scheduling/interruption handling; recovery-evidence limits and user controls; export/share pin and release conditions; reachability reconciliation/verification; interaction with the unapproved pointer mechanism; and acceptable technical verification evidence remain open.

This is a provisional retention policy, not approval of a storage engine, commit protocol, pointer durability or implementation. ## 17. Provisional low-storage safety policy (2026-10-30)

The project owner approved a **fail-without-replacement low-storage policy** for Save and Restore:

- If storage is insufficient to safely complete Save, fail without replacing the existing committed profile.
- If storage is insufficient to safely complete Restore, fail without partially replacing the existing committed profile; preserve P0.4 all-or-nothing Restore and explicit Save behavior.
- Do not delete or sacrifice the active generation, the protected predecessor, pinned export/share artifacts or protected recovery evidence merely to make space.
- A failed pre-commit operation leaves the last demonstrably committed profile authoritative. Do not report Save/Restore success unless the required durable commit is established.
- Preserve the distinction between an interrupted pre-commit operation and corruption of committed data. Staging cleanup is allowed only when demonstrably safe and not protected by recovery or active-operation requirements.

The exact storage budget/capacity estimates/reserves; exhaustion behavior at individual commit steps; pointer ordering; cleanup timing/retry/interruption; approved P0.2 low-storage communication; and verification requirements for process death, write failure and low-storage remain unresolved. No P0.2 UI, storage mechanism, durable pointer protocol or technical verification is approved by this policy.
