### 3. [P0.5_STATE_STORAGE_AND_RECOVERY.md](P0.5_STATE_STORAGE_AND_RECOVERY.md)

- **Version/status:** No numbered version declared; dated 2026-10-30. `PROPOSED — FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS; NO STORAGE SELECTION APPROVED`.
- **Proposed choices:** The owner provisionally prefers immutable saved generations with immutable manifests and write-once app-private blobs through a durable active-generation reference; this remains direction only. Exactly one previously committed predecessor is the normal-operation retention target, not a hard maximum during interruption, investigation or protected lifetimes. Before Save/Restore, estimate the complete candidate, temporary writes, protected generations/assets, active export/share pins, recovery evidence and reserve; estimates are advisory. Cleanup is idempotent and requires applicable commit establishment, complete protected-reference reconciliation and proof each candidate is unreferenced/unprotected. Low storage uses fail-without-replacement and preserves the committed profile/protected roots; success requires established durable commit. The owner also approved two-stage integrity policy: startup validates references/manifest/schema/profile metadata/asset records; each asset's size, SHA-256 and applicable P0.4 checks must pass before use. Metadata validity is not full-profile verification. Keep candidates isolated and require explicit replacement. Other representations remain alternatives.
- **Sources/evidence:** Strong official constraints: [AtomicFile](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/util/AtomicFile) is single-file and has no locking; [DataStore guide](https://developer.android.com/topic/architecture/datastore) describes one-object updates; [ZIP traversal guidance](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/risks/zip-path-traversal) supports strict path handling. P0.4 is the binding archive/state source.
- **Compatibility/licensing:** DataStore 1.3.0 and Room 2.8.5 are version snapshots only; license, exact artifact metadata and API-31 dependency closure still require selection-time review. No final store is selected; immutable generations are a provisional preferred direction only.
- **Open questions/risks:** Durability of active-reference/journal transitions, file/directory sync behavior, concurrency, cleanup after death, staging space and deterministic old-or-new recovery. The owner provisionally requires fail-closed handling, two-stage integrity validation, and low-storage failure without replacement, but detection/classification, last-durable-commit determination, pointer-slot handling, recovery-evidence limits, P0.2 failure communication, verification timing/caching/failure handling/access-path coverage and startup performance remain unresolved. For retention, normal-operation target is one predecessor, but budget/limits, low-storage capacity/reserves and commit-step behavior, cleanup scheduling/retry/interruption, pin/release conditions, reachability verification and interaction with the pointer remain open. Recovery after a proven pre-commit Save interruption is distinct from fallback after committed-data corruption. Both Android-managed migration channels are excluded by owner policy, but version-specific configuration and exclusion verification remain future obligations. AtomicFile/DataStore/Room alone cannot provide the cross-resource guarantee.
- **P0.1–P0.4 conflicts:** None found. This supports P0.1 FR-06/P0.2 S02/P0.4 whole-profile replacement and rejected-key policy. **Related IDs:** P0.5-04/05/06.
