## 2. Design vision and visual principles

Present a professional identity with the confidence and clarity of a well-made physical business card. My Card gets the strongest composition: cover, portrait, name and professional details, with generous space and restrained depth. Maintenance screens use the same typography, surfaces and alignment but less visual prominence. The result should feel native, readable and personal without decorative dashboards, glowing panels, heavy gradients or glass effects.

Material 3 is the interaction/design foundation. Approved dimensions below are VCard design values, not a claim to reproduce every current Material default. Use labeled actions, familiar selection, standard bars, sheets and dialogs. Do not let styling imply a new destination, editable template or extra capability.

## 3. Dark-first direction and optional light appearance

Use the approved cool slate dark canvas (`#101418`), slightly lighter surface (`#191E24`) and raised surfaces (`#222A33`), with readable neutral text (`#F2F4F8`). Avoid pure black across every surface so boundaries remain legible without heavy shadows. Dark is the initial product direction already required by the baseline; the exact palette is approved here.

Light appearance uses a near-white canvas (`#F7F9FC`), white surface and pale slate raised surface (`#EAF0F6`) with dark text (`#19212B`). Preserve hierarchy, action order, labels and focus order across themes. A theme change must never alter contact export or visibility settings. N05 previews draft appearance, while sharing and My Card use saved preferences until explicit Save succeeds. No wallpaper-derived/dynamic accent option or automatic theme mode is added.

## 4. Surfaces, hierarchy and card composition

Use `color.background` for the canvas, `color.surface` for ordinary content, and `color.surfaceRaised` for management groups, sheets and dialogs. The business card uses surface plus an optional subtle decorative outline; interactive boundaries use stronger `color.outline`. Distinguish surfaces primarily by fill, spacing and shape, with minimal light-theme shadow. Surface contrast alone is not used to identify required controls.

Within N01, order: cover imagery, portrait, full name, title/organization, biography/location, contact actions, professional links and conditional public-key download. Omit absent/hidden optional content and close its spacing. Keep identity/contact text on an opaque surface rather than over unpredictable photos. Use one card, not a carousel. A QR entry action is outside the identity artwork and selects the existing Share root. The card is not itself an unlabeled button.

## 5. Typography and readability

Use the approved Android system sans-serif family (Roboto where supplied) with no downloadable typeface, font picker or custom novelty font. Approved identity size is 32sp/40sp at medium weight, screen titles 24sp/32sp, sections 20sp/28sp, body/inputs 16sp/24sp, supporting and action labels 14sp/20sp, primary navigation labels 12sp/16sp. Token tables define all roles.

Use sentence case and stable labels. Name, essential data, errors and privacy notices wrap without ellipsis; never squeeze type to force one line. Navigation labels remain visible. URLs/emails/public-key summaries can wrap at safe visual boundaries without changing the underlying saved/copied/exported value. No arbitrary field length or data validation rule is established here.

## 6. Curated accents and semantic colors

Use the three approved fixed coordinated accent families: **Sapphire** (default), **Jade**, **Amethyst**, each with theme-specific primary/on-primary/container pairs. Secondary colors remain neutral slate. Accent choices change visual roles, not layouts or features. No free color editor, extra templates or font choices.

Use the accent for primary actions, selected navigation/mode/appearance options, meaningful links and focus treatment. Error and success colors remain semantically fixed regardless of accent. Each selected option has a check/label/semantic selected state; color alone never communicates the choice. Exact hexadecimal roles and computed examples are in the token document.

## 7. Icons and actions

Use one coherent native-style icon vocabulary: outlined for ordinary affordances, filled/stronger selected state where useful. Contact actions have visible text (Call, Message, Email, Website), consistent leading icons and at least 48dp targets. Icons are typically 24dp. A chevron indicates navigation; external-launch semantics identify external handoffs without adding a confirmation. Do not render a chevron on a noninteractive row.

Use filled accent for the principal action, tonal/outlined for peers and text for low-emphasis actions. Destructive replacement/discard uses explicit wording and error-color text where appropriate; Keep editing remains visually preferred in D01. Missing content/handlers follows P0.2 rather than presenting fake functionality. A known failure uses inline explanation/retry at the caller.

## 8. Navigation-bar visual behavior

N01–N03 share the same three labeled primary items: My Card, Share, Profile. Selected item uses an accent-container indicator with a selected icon/text state; inactive items use readable secondary text. Use a nominal 80dp content height above system bottom insets; height grows for text and content. No badges, extra tabs, floating navigation or Settings item.

N04–N06 use an Up action and hide the primary bar as approved. Screen titles identify the current task. Forms provide explicit Save in an accessible action area; Back/Up still invokes the approved dirty-state guard. Primary root selection does not become a chronological tab stack. Styling never changes system Back behavior or caller context.

## 9. Spacing, shape and elevation

Use a 4dp-based scale with 8dp major rhythm, nominal page gutter 20dp, 24dp section separation and 16dp ordinary surface padding. Text and controls align on shared start/end anchors. Use 24dp business-card corners, 16dp group corners, 12dp controls and 28dp top sheet/dialog corners. Rounded buttons may use a pill silhouette; their height remains content-driven.

Prefer flat content; nominal 0dp canvas, 1dp business-card light shadow, 3dp transient surfaces and no heavy dark-mode shadows. In dark mode, tonal separation does most of the work. Selected/focused controls need visible indicators independent of shadow. These are visual elevation intents; exact platform shadow/compositing behavior remains P0.5.

## 10. Profile and cover imagery

Use the approved wide 3:1 cover viewport, profile portrait 88dp in a circular frame and a 4dp surface-colored separation edge. Portrait can overlap the lower cover edge without putting identity text over imagery. Center-crop the display viewport only; do not add crop controls, source-image processing features or destructive edits. At short heights/large text, reduce decorative cover presence and place the portrait in normal flow to protect information space.

If imagery is absent or cannot load, use a neutral geometric placeholder without invented initials/data or a fake person. User-provided cover art is decorative; portrait semantics avoid repeating the full identity block. Selection/editing stays exclusively in N04 via the approved system handoff. N05 links there through existing D01 when dirty. Cancel preserves the previous saved image.

## 11. QR presentation and accompanying text

N02 opens in Contact QR mode and immediately displays a valid offline code with readable included data. Keep QR modules black on an opaque white square in both themes and all accent choices; no gradient, logo overlay, rounded modules, decorative tint or image over the code. Preserve the later approved encoder's quiet zone exactly; an outer white visual pad is additional, not a substitute. Panel preferred side is 280dp, constrained by available width, with no fixed scan guarantee or payload limit selected here.

Mode labels read **Contact QR — works offline** and **Web-profile QR — recipient needs internet**. Distinguish them with label, icon and selected state, never color alone. Contact mode includes actual supported included values and voluntary Review contact data → S01. The code has no initial blocking confirmation. .vcf and contact QR image save/share require S01 and explicit action-specific confirmation before export generation/handoff. Relevant saved changes invalidate stale previews/artifacts.

Web mode shows its URL and a persistent hosted-content independence notice. Missing/invalid URL produces explanatory copy/configuration link, not an active fake QR. Never describe web sharing as controlled by local export switches. QR size/encoding/limits and safe module dimensions remain P0.4/P0.5; recipient scan/import checks remain P0.6. This specification adds no full-screen QR or zoom destination.

## 12. Forms and visibility controls

N04 groups fields with clear section headings, persistent labels, values and nearby supporting/error text. Filled or outlined text-input treatment uses neutral surfaces and a strong active/error border. Input text is at least body size; labels do not replace field names with placeholder-only hints. Exact required fields, input validity and supported link lists remain P0.4.

For each supported field, present two separately labeled controls: Show on card and Include in contact export. At large text, stack controls below the field; do not compress them into ambiguous eye/share icons. Display the field name in each control's accessible name, state and support explanation. Defaults/non-hideable essentials and format support remain unresolved. Never expose export-hidden values in the Share preview as a styling convenience.

## 13. Bottom sheets and dialog

S01 and S02 share raised-surface fill, top corner radius, visible drag handle (decorative), title and scrollable body. Provide a labeled dismissal route; swipe/Back retains the approved cancellation contract. Give action areas enough bottom/system padding and allow full-height sheet expansion as a state of the same sheet, not a new destination. Each title explains the current operation.

S01 action-specific copy changes between Create and share .vcf, Save contact QR and Share contact QR; voluntary review has no accidental export action. S02 appears only after validation and explicitly names replacement. D01 is the sole reusable confirmation dialog: title Unsaved changes?, body about leaving the originating editor, actions Keep editing and Discard changes. For N05's pending image link, body explains that Discard restores saved appearance then opens Edit Profile. Dismissal means Keep editing. Do not add a Save action, second dialog or automatic save.

## 14. Feedback and states

Empty/missing optional fields collapse cleanly on My Card. No saved profile uses the existing guided N04 state. Use stable labeled progress within the caller; never show an old QR as current while updating. Field errors combine icon, message and outline; error color is not sufficient by itself. Successful local save/write uses a brief announced message; external return never means delivery/import succeeded.

Keep errors and recovery actions in the content, not only in a fleeting notification. Restore validation failure remains N06 and does not open S02; committed replacement uses clear progress and the approved temporary navigation restriction. Unknown interruption outcomes use factual status rather than a success checkmark. Recovery semantics and draft persistence remain deferred technical dependencies. No new error/loading/success screen is added.

## 15. Accessibility and large text

Apply at least 4.5:1 text contrast throughout the chosen solid color pairs, even where large-text exceptions could apply; meaningful control boundaries/focus indicators target at least 3:1 against adjacent fills. Use at least 48dp square targets, enlarged naturally when text wraps. These choices align with [Android accessibility guidance](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps) and use [WCAG contrast criteria](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum) as design checks, not an app-conformance claim.

Screen-reader order follows content order. Mark headings, selected navigation and switches correctly; decorative art/icons are not extra stops. QR has a readable text alternative; focused actions announce purpose/mode and external handoff when relevant. Sheet/dialog focus moves to meaningful title/content and returns to the invoker. Errors associate with fields, and progress/status is announced without stealing focus repeatedly.

At enlarged text/display size, wrap headings and button labels, stack contact actions and visibility controls, grow bars/rows/sheets and scroll. Never fix control height to crop text, scale fonts down, hide required action labels or put confirmations behind the keyboard. The approved design-review sizes are 100%, 150% and 200% font scale at compact widths; these are unrendered design checks to perform later, not device test results or new release criteria.

## 16. Motion and animation

Use brief, functional feedback: 120ms pressed/selection color, 180ms fade for local visual-state changes and 240ms sheet/dialog enter/exit intent. Keep navigation gestures/system-managed activity motion native. Do not animate QR modules, pulse important text, add parallax or continuously move the card. Theme preview should remain stable and must not delay Save or QR presentation.

Respect system reduced-motion/disabled-animation settings: remove spatial/decorative transitions and use immediate states or minimal crossfades only where permitted. No new in-app motion toggle. Approved easing is specified in tokens; architecture/platform feasibility remains P0.5, actual comfort/accessibility verification P0.6.

## 17. Responsive layouts for the target Pixel

The Pixel model/OS and usable display dimensions remain unspecified. Use **360dp, 412dp and 480dp available portrait widths as design review cases**, not asserted device specifications. Subtract actual system bars, cutouts and keyboard from usable space. Center primary content with the approved 600dp maximum width; on compact windows use full available width with 20dp gutters (16dp when necessary). Do not add a tablet navigation rail or master/detail destination.

In landscape or split-screen, prefer a single vertically scrolling column, shorter cover and no forced one-screen card fit. The QR panel remains square, with all supporting text/actions reachable by scrolling; do not shrink below the future approved scan-safe geometry to fit height. Keyboard-visible editors scroll the focused field/support text above the keyboard and keep Save reachable without overlapping it. Final device fit remains unverified.
