## P0.3-01 — Palette and curated accents

- **Description:** Neutral dark/light surfaces and coordinated accent role pairs.
- **Approved choice:** Slate dark-first and near-white light palettes; Sapphire default, Jade and Amethyst alternatives; semantic error/success fixed; no dynamic wallpaper palette. The unchanged Sapphire family is the chosen interpretation of the original restrained electric-blue direction, which specifies no exact hex/saturation value.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Warmer neutral palette or a smaller two-accent set, each requiring recalculation of paired contrast.
- **Advantages:** Restrained identity, limited choices, predictable contrast and consistent semantics.
- **Disadvantages:** Fixed palettes offer less personalization; neutral scheme needs imagery/type for character. Alpha/pressed combinations remain unverified.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-05; P0.1-06/P0.1-11; P0.2-07. Hex values/pair calculations in tokens.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-02 — System typography and hierarchy

- **Description:** One readable type family with identity-led scale.
- **Approved choice:** Android system sans-serif, no new font dependency; identity32/40sp, titles24/32sp, sections20/28sp, body/input16/24sp, support/actions14/20sp, nav12/16sp; wrapping instead of shrinking/clipping.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Slightly smaller identity scale after fit review; different weights within the same system family.
- **Advantages:** Native character, predictable fallback and strong hierarchy without font-selection features.
- **Disadvantages:** Actual Pixel glyph metrics and long-name/large-text fit remain unverified; system font availability may vary.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-01/FR-05; P0.1-06/P0.1-11; P0.2 accessibility contract.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-03 — Business-card and imagery composition

- **Description:** Make My Card the distinctive presentation surface.
- **Approved choice:** One 24dp-radius opaque card, wide3: 1 cover viewport, 88dp portrait, identity below art and restrained depth; display crop only, missing imagery neutral.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Portrait fully below cover without overlap; shallower cover viewport for more compact presentation.
- **Advantages:** Professional emphasis, readable identity independent of arbitrary photos, no generic settings-card appearance.
- **Disadvantages:** Decorative image area costs vertical space; viewport crop may obscure a user's preferred photo composition. Large text requires reflow.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-01/FR-05; P0.1-02/P0.1-03/P0.1-06; N01, P0.2-07 single editor preserved.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-04 — Icon and action treatment

- **Description:** Consistent labeled contact/navigation/external actions.
- **Approved choice:** Coherent native-style 24dp icons, visible sentence-case labels, filled principal action and tonal/outlined peers; controls have >=48dp targets.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** All contact actions as full-width rows; less prominent tonal principal button while retaining clear labels.
- **Advantages:** Readable purpose, familiar hierarchy and no reliance on icon recognition alone.
- **Disadvantages:** Labels use more space; wrapped buttons/rows require layout review.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-01/FR-04; P0.2-09/P0.2-11; external handoffs remain native and conditional controls remain conditional.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-05 — Bars and selected navigation visuals

- **Description:** Style the already-approved three-root and secondary-screen structure.
- **Approved choice:** Always-labeled 80dp nominal primary bar with tonal selected indicator; simple 64dp nominal app bar; secondary Up/Save and no primary bar; dimensions grow.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Slightly different indicator silhouette or bar tonal depth; no alternate navigation architecture proposed.
- **Advantages:** Clear location and consistent secondary editing focus.
- **Disadvantages:** Persistent labeled bar consumes height; large text may enlarge it further.
- **Requirements relationship:** P0.2-01/P0.2-02/P0.2-04/P0.2-10; N01–N06 and Back behavior unchanged.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-06 — Spatial rhythm, corners and depth

- **Description:** Reusable spacing/shape/elevation language.
- **Approved choice:** 4dp scale/8dp major rhythm, 20dp page gutters, 16dp ordinary padding, 24dp sections; 12/16/24/28dp shape roles; tonal separation with minimal shadows.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):**16dp normal gutters or squarer group shapes while keeping target sizes/readability.
- **Advantages:** Cohesion across card and maintenance surfaces; restrained premium depth.
- **Disadvantages:** Larger gaps increase scrolling; dark subtle surfaces require explicit interactive boundaries.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-05 and accessibility; P0.2's six-screen inventory/large-text contract.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-07 — QR panel and sharing hierarchy

- **Description:** Visual separation of offline contact QR, online web QR and action previews.
- **Approved choice:** Opaque black/white square, intact quiet zone, 280dp preferred panel, readable summary/URL and clearly labeled modes. Immediate valid contact QR, voluntary presentation review and mandatory action-specific export review remain unchanged.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Different preferred panel size subject to later scan constraints; alternate placement of the same summary/actions without hiding them.
- **Advantages:** Strong contrast, clear offline/online distinction and transparent export content.
- **Disadvantages:** White panel is visually bright in dark mode; payload geometry may limit sizing. Actual scanning remains unverified.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-03/FR-04; P0.2-05/P0.2-06 and Clarification A; no encoding/limit/default decision made.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-08 — Form and independent visibility presentation

- **Description:** Readable long-form editing with separate sharing controls.
- **Approved choice:** Persistent field labels, 16sp input, field-adjacent help/errors, two named privacy controls per supported field; stacked layout when constrained. N04 remains the only image-selection location.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Filled rather than outlined field treatment using the same tokens; different visual grouping of the same approved fields.
- **Advantages:** Independent privacy meaning and usable long-form errors; one reusable editor.
- **Disadvantages:** Paired controls lengthen the form; final support/default/required-field rules remain P0.4.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-01/FR-02/FR-03; P0.2-03/P0.2-04/P0.2-07 and Clarification B.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-09 — Sheet and dialog styling

- **Description:** Shared review/confirmation presentation without extra modals.
- **Approved choice:** Raised opaque surfaces, 28dp modal corners, viewport-aware scroll and stacked actions; S01 action-specific export labels, S02 explicit replacement, D01 Keep editing/Discard only.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** More compact modal corner radius or padding, while retaining all text/actions and the same surface inventory.
- **Advantages:** Review context remains visible; consistent cancellation and clear consequences.
- **Disadvantages:** Long data/large text may expand sheets to full height; keyboard/focus interaction needs later validation.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-02/FR-03/FR-06; P0.2-02/P0.2-06/P0.2-07/P0.2-08/P0.2-10. Exactly two sheets and one dialog.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-10 — Feedback and conditional-state visuals

- **Description:** Consistent validation, progress, success/error and unavailable states.
- **Approved choice:** Inline text+icon messages, persistent error/retry paths, factual local-success feedback, labeled indeterminate progress when unknown; stale codes removed from active use.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Different neutral/error notice fill weight or progress placement within the same caller.
- **Advantages:** Honest outcomes and recoverable failures without extra screens or color-only status.
- **Disadvantages:** Persistent messages can increase content height; interruption outcomes depend on later mechanisms.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-01/FR-02/FR-04/FR-06; P0.2-08/P0.2-09/P0.2-12. No new recovery/backup-history feature.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-11 — Restrained motion

- **Description:** Functional feedback respecting system reduced-motion behavior.
- **Approved choice:**120ms feedback, 180ms local fade, 240ms modal intent; no QR/module animation, parallax or continuous card motion. Remove spatial/decorative motion when system preferences require it; no app toggle.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Shorter durations or near-instant app transitions while retaining native external/system behavior.
- **Advantages:** Polished feedback without distraction; QR and action availability remain immediate.
- **Disadvantages:** Actual motion comfort/performance and platform preference integration remain unverified.
- **Requirements relationship:** FR-05; P0.1-06/P0.1-11; P0.2 accessibility/feedback behavior. P0.5 integration and P0.6 tests deferred.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.

## P0.3-12 — Responsive and accessible layout rules

- **Description:** Fit the target Pixel without inventing its model/dimensions.
- **Approved choice:** Single-column layout, 600dp maximum content width, flexible gutters, wrapping/stacking at large text, inset/keyboard awareness and visible focus; review360/412/480dp widths and100/150/200% font scales later.
- **Historical alternatives (not selected):** Narrower max width or different spacing compression after actual device review; no new rail/master-detail destination.
- **Advantages:** Keeps labels, data and actions reachable; accommodates long content and split/landscape windows.
- **Disadvantages:** More scrolling and larger bars at high scales; no rendered/device evidence yet. QR scan-safe fit remains dependent on P0.4–P0.6.
- **Requirements relationship:** P0.1-11/FR-05; all P0.2 screen-reader/large-text/Back contracts. These are design review cases, not new approved release gates.
- **Owner approval:** Granted by the project owner on 2026-10-29; guideline reconciliation complete. **Status:** APPROVED.
