# VCard Native V1.1 — Requirements & Acceptance Criteria

**Document status:** Approved — Milestone 1.1.h signed off by project owner.  
**Design status:** Milestone 1.1.g approved.  
**Feasibility:** Milestone 1.1.f planning assessment approved; implementation and device verification pending.  
**Planning status:** Milestone 1.1.1 and submilestones 1.1.a–1.1.h are approved and complete. Milestone 1.1.2 implementation preparation is next; implementation has not started.  
**Baseline:** Preserve shipped V1.0.0 features and data. This document supplements rather than replaces V1.0.0 documentation.

## 1. Release scope

V1.1 includes five features: (a) Quick Share; (b) launcher long-press primary QR shortcut; (c) automatic full-name generation; (d) website and social-profile presentation improvements; (e) resizable Quick Card home-screen widget. Milestone 1.1.2 is implementation preparation; feature implementation has not started. Any subsequent implementation remains subject to incremental review and V1.0.0 compatibility safeguards.

## 2. Cross-cutting invariants

- Preserve the existing V1.0.0 single phone-number and single email-address fields. Do not introduce multi-number or multi-email support in V1.1.
- Preserve existing saved profile values, existing primary QR, and current contact export settings and functionality.
- Social profile improvements remain in V1.1, but social profiles must not appear in Quick Share selections or Quick Share QR/vCard outputs.
- Quick Share is a deliberate sharing action with its own selection state, not a global privacy-setting change. Its selections do not consult or modify existing `includeInContactExport` settings.
- QR and vCard core functions remain usable offline.
- Saved-profile information is the single source for the card, QR, exports, and widget; uncommitted editor changes must not leak into outputs.
- Apply backward-compatible data changes, accessible touch targets, and existing V1.0 visual language.

## 3. Feature 1.1.a — Quick Share

### User experience

- Replace the green **Show QR** action on My Card with **Quick Share**; preserve a separate route to the existing primary full-profile QR.
- Two full-screen steps: **Select Information** → **Review & Share**.
- Automatically include the saved Full Name in every Quick Share contact. Full Name is not a selectable option. If the saved Full Name is unavailable, do not fabricate an identity; show a safe state requiring attention.
- Show only populated saved values for these four independently selectable additional fields: `ProfileField.ProfessionalTitle`, `ProfileField.Organization`, `ProfileField.Phone`, and `ProfileField.Email`. These identifiers are confirmed in the existing Android `ProfileField` enum. Do not add any other eligible field.
- Do not show Website, social profiles, or structured-name components as options. Do not include Website, social profiles, structured-name components, internal identifiers, settings, metadata, or application-only data in Quick Share QR or vCard output, regardless of existing export visibility settings.
- The V1.1 exact-URL-preservation requirement continues to apply to ordinary saved Website functionality and Feature 1.1.d. Website URL compatibility is not a Quick Share V1.1.0 blocker because Website is excluded from Quick Share.
- Quick Share uses only its own confirmed field selections as the output filter. Existing `includeInContactExport` settings do not filter Quick Share QR or vCard, and Quick Share never changes those settings. The primary full-profile QR and ordinary contact exports continue to follow their existing behavior and settings.
- First use starts with no additional fields selected. Subsequent uses restore the last **confirmed additional-field** selection. Save selections only when sharing is initiated; cancel/discard leaves prior saved selections unchanged.
- Confirm the selected additional fields when the user starts either Quick Share QR or **Share Contact**. Both output formats include saved Full Name plus the same confirmed additional-field selection. Provide **Select All** and **Clear All** for the four additional fields only. Require at least one populated additional field to be selected before continuing.
- Review displays the saved Full Name and only the selected populated additional fields from the saved profile. **Generate QR** and **Share Contact** use that same contact information. Returning from QR or Android sharing returns to Review; **Done** returns to My Card.
- When no additional eligible fields are populated, show the approved informative empty state.
- QR over payload capacity is disabled with a clear warning; valid vCard sharing remains available.

### Acceptance checks

- A profile with one phone/email displays one selectable item each, not repeated-entry controls; saved Full Name is displayed automatically and is not selectable.
- Quick Share QR and vCard contain saved Full Name and only confirmed selected additional fields, with no social-profile or structured-name-component values.
- Website is unavailable in Quick Share selection, is not persisted as a Quick Share selection, and does not appear in Quick Share vCard or QR even when the saved profile has a Website.
- Cancelling does not overwrite remembered selections; initiating sharing does.
- Primary QR and ordinary contact exports remain unaffected.
- Over-limit QR shows a warning and does not block an otherwise valid vCard.
- A missing saved Full Name is handled safely without inventing identity data and requires attention.

## 4. Feature 1.1.b — Long-Press QR Shortcut

- Register a launcher shortcut labeled **Show My QR Code**.
- Open the existing primary full-profile QR directly, bypassing Quick Share and ignoring its saved selections.
- Back/Close after shortcut launch returns to the Android launcher; normal in-app QR Back/Close returns to My Card.
- Handle both cold starts and shortcut intents delivered to an already running activity.
- Missing profile routes to existing setup/recovery rather than crashing.

**Acceptance checks:** cold/warm launches, Back and Close, QR content parity with primary QR, missing-profile behavior.

## 5. Feature 1.1.c — Automatic Full-Name Generation

- Show a read-only but selectable/copyable live preview above First/Middle/Last name fields.
- Generate First → Middle → Last, skipping blank components; normalize whitespace while preserving Unicode, punctuation, case, and multi-word component values.
- Validate up to 200 Unicode code points per First, Middle, and Last component. The generated Full Name may contain up to 602 Unicode code points, including up to two separator spaces. Never silently truncate; normalize whitespace and skip blank components before joining.
- Require at least one nonblank name component for new profiles or intentionally edited structured names.
- Preserve an existing V1.0.0 Full Name until a structured name component is intentionally changed. Do not infer or split legacy names.
- If structured edits are undone, restore the original legacy full-name preview. If a component is cleared and the change is saved, omit it from the generated full name.
- Commit changes only on successful Save Profile. Unrelated profile edits must not rewrite the legacy Full Name. Confirm before discarding unsaved editor changes.
- Export vCard FN and N consistently with the stored name data; never fabricate missing structured components.
- Do not introduce a name-order preference.

**V1.0 compatibility clarification:** The existing V1.0.0 validator applies a 160-code-point limit to Full Name and structured name components. V1.1 generated-name behavior supersedes that limit for those name values as needed to support the approved 200/602 limits. Do not raise limits for unrelated fields by implication. Implementation review must inspect the shared validator's other fields and preserve their existing limits unless separately approved. Existing saved Full Name values remain unchanged unless structured-name changes are intentionally made and successfully saved.

**Acceptance checks:** legacy profile untouched, unrelated edit, intentional name change, edit-and-undo, invalid empty/overlong components, Unicode, save failure, discard, FN/N export.

## 6. Feature 1.1.d — Professional Website & Social Links

All 13 approved design decisions are retained:

1. Website displays underlined **Visit Website**; entire row is tappable; underlying URL preserved.
2. Recognized social platforms display icons only, without platform names, URLs, or View Profile labels.
3. Social icons occupy a single horizontally scrollable row.
4. Unsupported platforms display underlined hyperlink text, not a generic icon.
5. Unsupported-platform label is the user-entered platform name.
6. A partially visible next item indicates horizontal scrolling when applicable.
7. Save newly entered website and social URLs exactly as entered; do not validate, normalize, rewrite, or automatically prepend `https://`. Preserve existing V1.0.0 saved URL text unchanged.
8. Show a brief, non-intrusive error if Android cannot open a link.
9. Display a separate icon for every saved social profile, including duplicates of one platform.
10. Preserve the user's saved profile order.
11. Use monochrome icons consistent with the VCard Native theme.
12. Hide an empty social row; retain Website if present; hide Online Presence if both are empty.
13. Provide minimum 48×48 dp touch targets with compact visible icons.

**Compatibility:** Preserve and migrate existing LinkedIn, GitHub, and Other Professional Link data as needed; maintain their inclusion in the primary QR and existing contact-export flows according to existing behavior/settings. Social profiles are excluded from Quick Share only.

**URL storage and opening boundary:** Exact user-entered text is the stored value. Link opening may derive a temporary URI candidate, but it must not rewrite stored data. Reject malformed or unsafe/unsupported URI handling at that boundary rather than passing unsafe input to Android. The exact accepted scheme allowlist requires implementation review; no automatic scheme insertion is permitted. Preserve offline-first storage and presentation.

**Social-profile migration:** Add an additive, backward-compatible migration from the current three scalar fields to an ordered, repeatable profile collection. Preserve each populated LinkedIn, GitHub, and Other Professional Link value, its existing contact-export visibility preference, and its saved URL text. Each migrated entry inherits the `includeInContactExport` preference of its source field; do not replace that preference during migration. The current model has no saved social ordering; migrate legacy entries in deterministic order: LinkedIn, GitHub, then Other Professional Link. Preserve user ordering for entries in the new collection, including multiple entries for one platform and repeated URLs where allowed by the approved data model. Preserve unknown or unsupported platform names without dropping them. Migration must be idempotent and avoid duplicate entries when already-migrated data is loaded. V1.1 profile import and backup/restore must preserve the collection and V1.0.0 backups must remain readable. Preserve primary QR and ordinary export behavior. Upgrade compatibility is required; V1.0.0 downgrade compatibility with V1.1-written data is not promised. The existing migration boundaries are `ProfileCodec` for private profile JSON and `BackupDataCodec` for backup profile data; their current source schema is `AppData.CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 3`.

**Recognized-platform icon allowlist: OWNER DECISION REQUIRED.** The current approved design documents and source do not define a complete recognized-platform list. Do not infer one from examples or generic icons. Until approved, icon rendering cannot be finalized; unsupported platform names remain user-provided text links.

**Acceptance checks:** migration without lost links, duplicate platforms, order, recognized/unsupported display, row scrolling, tap targets, URL preservation, failed link handling, visibility rules, existing exports.

## 7. Feature 1.1.e — Resizable Quick Card Widget

- Android home-screen widget, responsive target sizes **2×1 compact**, **2×2 standard**, and **4×2 expanded**.
- Compact layout prioritizes fitting beside the user's existing UV widget; launcher-specific cell sizes mean exact placement cannot be guaranteed until tested on the user's Pixel launcher.
- Show saved profile photo or initials; use generic person icon if initials unavailable.
- Show saved full name and professional title, up to two lines each where space permits, with ellipsis as necessary. Compact layout may prioritize legibility over displaying every detail.
- Tap anywhere to open My Card. When no profile exists, show **Create Your Card** and route to the appropriate creation experience.
- Request widget refresh after successful profile save; use last saved data, never unsaved edits.
- Follow Android light/dark appearance; handle missing images, process death, and resizing gracefully.

**Acceptance checks:** add/resize widget on Pixel launcher at 2×1, 2×2, 4×2 when supported; visual clipping; name/title overflow; image fallbacks; empty state; tap behavior; theme; update after save; app process termination.

## 8. Engineering risks and validation gates

| Risk | Mitigation / required verification |
|---|---|
| Social-profile storage evolution | Backward-compatible migration; upgrade a populated V1.0.0 profile and verify links and export output. |
| Quick Share vs existing sharing | Isolated selection persistence and filtered export; regression-test primary QR and existing vCard settings. |
| Legacy name rewriting and length limits | Explicit structured-name change detection and save-only commit; test 200-code-point components, 602-code-point generated Full Name, unrelated edits, and preserve non-name limits. |
| URL storage and launch safety | Preserve exact stored text; review the explicit safe-scheme/URI-opening boundary and test malformed or unsupported URIs without rewriting data. |
| Recognized social icon platforms | Owner must approve the icon allowlist before implementing the recognized-platform presentation. |
| Shortcut task stack | Test cold/warm intent delivery and context-specific Back/Close. |
| Launcher-specific widget sizes | Test actual supported cells and resize behavior on target Pixel; adapt layout to measured space. |
| QR capacity | Validate byte limits and vCard fallback on oversized payloads. |

**Gate to implementation:** Milestone 1.1.h and the approved clarifications in this document are the planning baseline. Resolve feature-specific open decisions before implementing the affected behavior, including the social icon allowlist before recognized-platform icons and the safe URI scheme policy before link opening. No V1.1 feature implementation, compilation, tests, or device verification is claimed. **Gate to release:** compile/build, automated tests where appropriate, physical Pixel testing, offline QR/vCard compatibility, upgrade/regression testing, and release-signing verification, including verification of the signing-file ownership and handling item tracked in the V1.1 roadmap.
