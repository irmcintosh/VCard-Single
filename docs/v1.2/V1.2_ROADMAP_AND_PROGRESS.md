- **High:** Appearance serialization and backup compatibility, particularly new style and Minimal visibility settings.
- **Medium:** QR MediaStore and FileProvider image handling; social brand icon assets; RemoteViews widget accent behavior; preview/export parity; card style accessibility.
- **Pending:** Exact existing colors/defaults; proposed new colors; empty Minimal selections; QR sizing/scan tests; final Horizon differentiation.
